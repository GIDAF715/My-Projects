About for Apple Company

Apple Inc. is one of the world's largest technology companies, famous for its premium consumer electronics, software, and services.


  • Founders: Founded by Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak in 1976 inside a small garage in California.

  • Core Products: Renowned globally for the iPhone, Mac computers, iPad, Apple Watch, and Apple Vision Pro.

  • Financial Status: Holds a massive market value of around $3.7 trillion, serving billions of active devices worldwide.

  • Headquarters: Located at the iconic, ring-shaped Apple Park in Cupertino, California.

  • Philosophy: Driven by the classic slogan "Think Different" and a focus on sleek, simple design.
